Hobbies & Interests

In the fast-paced world of work and responsibilities, it’s important for me to cultivate a well-rounded lifestyle that extends beyond the office. My life outside of work is a vibrant tapestry of interests and passions that keep me balanced, energised and creatively fulfilled.

Online Gaming & Physical Fitness

Maintaining a healthy lifestyle is a top priority for me, and I balance both mental and physical well‑being through exercise and online gaming. Regular workouts—whether it’s a morning run or a focused gym session—are complemented by immersive games like Overwatch, Call of Duty, V Rising, Minecraft, Wuthering Waves and Mobile Legends Bang Bang (MLBB). These games provide both entertainment and a mental challenge, helping me unwind and recharge after a busy day.

Online Gaming Fitness Well‑being

Drone Piloting & Nature Videography

I’ve developed a real passion for flying drones and capturing nature from the sky. Using a drone equipped with a camera, I love recording aerial footage of forests, cities and landscapes. This bird’s‑eye view reveals patterns and scenes you can’t see from the ground, and I try to use it to share the beauty of our environment and inspire a deeper appreciation for the planet.

Drones Nature Videography Aerial Footage

Drawing & Illustration

Drawing is my favourite analog medium for self‑expression. The simple act of putting pen or pencil to paper helps me translate thoughts and emotions into something tangible. Whether it’s a quick sketch to clear my mind or a more detailed piece, drawing is a cathartic, calming hobby that brings balance to a hectic schedule.

Sketching Illustration Creativity

Tech Enthusiast & IT Learning

Technology is not just part of my job—it’s also a genuine personal interest. I regularly dedicate time outside of work to learning something new in the IT world, whether that’s practising a new programming language, exploring emerging tools and technologies, or attending webinars and online events. This continuous learning keeps my skills sharp and my curiosity active.

IT & Programming Emerging Tech Lifelong Learning

Travel & Quality Time with Loved Ones

Travel is one of my favourite ways to broaden my horizons and create meaningful memories. Exploring new places with friends and family doesn’t just satisfy my sense of adventure—it also deepens our relationships through shared experiences, new cultures and moments that we can look back on together.

Travel Family & Friends Experiences

Cooking & Culinary Experiments

Cooking is much more than a daily task for me—it’s a creative adventure. I enjoy trying new recipes, experimenting with flavours, and turning simple ingredients into something special. It’s also a social hobby: sharing homemade food with friends and family creates warm, memorable moments around the table.

Cooking Food Experiments Shared Meals

Fiora Diaries – My YouTube Channel

Fiora Diaries is my YouTube channel and creative playground, where I share the things I love outside of work—from tech and AI experiments to art, gaming, food and everyday moments. It’s a relaxed, personal space where I document my hobbies, explore ideas, and connect with people who enjoy the same blend of creativity and curiosity.

I treat video as another storytelling medium, combining design, photography and editing to create simple, honest content. It’s less about perfection and more about learning, experimenting and having fun along the way.

YouTube Content Creation Storytelling

Entertainment: Music, Movies & TV

Music, movies and TV shows are a big part of my downtime. Music is a constant companion that helps me relax, focus or find inspiration. Films transport me to different worlds and perspectives, while TV series provide long‑form stories I can really get absorbed in. Together, they offer a comforting and creative escape.

Music Movies TV Series

Photography & Food Photography

Photography is one of my favourite ways to pause time and capture life’s small and big moments—from travel landscapes to everyday scenes. It’s like keeping a visual diary of the people, places and details that matter to me. I’m especially drawn to food photography, where I document my own homemade dishes from raw ingredients to final plating, combining my love for cooking and imagery.

Photography Food Photography Visual Storytelling

Graphic Designing

Beyond traditional drawing, I also enjoy expressing ideas through digital graphic design. It’s where artistry meets technology, allowing me to explore layouts, typography, colour and branding concepts. Whether I’m working on personal pieces or experimenting with new visual styles, graphic design keeps my creative energy flowing and gives me another dynamic outlet for expression.
