Championing Compassion: A Journey in Animal Welfare in Sri Lanka ❤️

I’ve spent time volunteering with animal welfare organisations in Sri Lanka, helping injured, abandoned, and lost animals get the care they deserve. From rescue and medical treatment to rehabilitation and reuniting pets with their families, I’ve seen first-hand how compassion can completely change an animal’s life. Through this work, I’ve also helped raise awareness about responsible pet care and adoption within local communities.

These experiences have inspired a long-term dream: to one day create my own animal welfare foundation, dedicated to giving every animal a safe home and a second chance. ❤️
