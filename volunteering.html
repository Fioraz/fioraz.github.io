Volunteering

Volunteering is one of the most meaningful parts of my life. My work in animal welfare has shown me how compassion, consistency and community can transform the lives of vulnerable animals.

Championing Compassion: Animal Welfare in Sri Lanka

I volunteer with animal welfare organisations in Sri Lanka, supporting injured, abandoned and lost animals so they can receive the care they deserve. This includes rescue assistance, basic care, helping with treatment, and reuniting pets with their families where possible.

Alongside hands‑on work, I help raise awareness in local communities about responsible pet care and adoption—encouraging people to see animals as family members who deserve safety, dignity and long‑term commitment.

Dreaming Ahead: A Future Animal Welfare Foundation

These experiences have inspired a long‑term goal: to create an animal welfare foundation in Sri Lanka that offers safe shelter, medical care and rehabilitation for vulnerable animals.

My vision is a dedicated space where every rescued animal has a second chance—supported by education, outreach and partnerships that promote compassionate, responsible care across local communities.
